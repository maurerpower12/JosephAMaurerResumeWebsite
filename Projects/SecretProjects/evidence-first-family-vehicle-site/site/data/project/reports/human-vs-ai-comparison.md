# Human-versus-AI Comparison

The independent recommendation was locked before this comparison.

## Agreements
- **Honda Pilot eligibility** — Pilot passes all hard filters and was deep-researched, but scores sixth because current IIHS test quality, reliability evidence and TCO are weaker than leading options.
- **CX-90 cargo** — Eliminated at low cost because published cargo is below the 16-cu-ft threshold.
- **XC90 cargo and row-3 child-seat limits** — Eliminated on cargo before expensive research.
- **EV first-model-year risk** — The independent EV finalist, EV9, receives only 1/10 reliability because first-year electrical/ICCU risk is material.

## Disagreements
- **Volkswagen Atlas row-3 LATCH** — Manual: Two row-3 anchor and tether positions. AI audit: IIHS maps both third-row positions as tether-only, so the Atlas fails the configured complete-LATCH definition. Impact: Definitive elimination; the manual shortlist would have admitted an ineligible vehicle.
- **Acura MDX NHTSA applicability** — Manual: Five-star NHTSA rating. AI audit: The exact 2024 SH-AWD configuration is shown as unrated; the configured unrated policy is not a pass. Impact: Definitive elimination for the exact drivetrain.
- **Ioniq 9 model-year scope** — Manual: Included despite first model year being 2026. AI audit: Outside the frozen 2023-2025 intersection and unavailable as a 2022-2025 used vehicle. Impact: Not eligible for this run.
- **Grand Highlander safety** — Manual: Retained as a functional hybrid despite lower safety rating. AI audit: Fails the exact-year IIHS award gate and therefore is not scoreable. Impact: Definitive elimination.
